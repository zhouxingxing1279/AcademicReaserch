## Mathematical audit: canonical second-order states
For `G(s)=n0/(s²+d1*s+d0)`, the **physical-rate** realization `x=(theta,theta_dot)` obeys `theta_dot=x2`, `x2_dot=-d0*theta-d1*x2+n0*u`. The **observable companion** realization `z=(theta,z2)` obeys `theta_dot=-d1*theta+z2`, `z2_dot=-d0*theta+n0*u`; hence `z2=theta_dot+d1*theta`. This is a coordinate identity, not a claim that every paper uses the latter realization. A rate-state label must be checked against the *printed equations* before mapping an IMU gyro measurement, state constraint, or terminal set. If physical rates are required, transform `theta_dot=z2-d1*theta`, including state/measurement maps and transformed disturbance sets.

**Physical-closure implication:** three virtual inputs `(Sx,Sy,Tz)` are not a four-rotor authority certificate. With finite inner-loop response, yaw authority and allocation/saturation constraints, upper-layer feasible `u` need not imply a feasible common four-rotor command. Hu's published 10-state example is not a drop-in 12-state plant nor an independent validation of rotor-rate feasibility. Its coefficient differences from Bouffard are an unresolved platform-identification question.
