## Original papers and evidence
1. Davi A. Santos and Constantino M. Lagoa, *Wayset-based guidance of multirotor aerial vehicles using robust tube-based model predictive control*, ISA Transactions 128 (2022), 123–135. DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isatra.2021.12.002 ; PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34980480/ ; Penn State record https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/wayset-based-guidance-of-multirotor-aerial-vehicles-using-robust-/ . **Abstract only, 2026-11-03**. Fixed-horizon tube MPC is **outer supervisory wayset guidance**; pre-existing stabilizing attitude and position inner controllers and time-scale separation yield a reduced-order discrete linear closed-loop translational model with bounded additive random disturbances and measurement noise. Abstract claims recursive feasibility, robust stability and guidance feasibility analysis plus realistic quadrotor numerical simulation. This does **not** establish motor-level constraints, full 12-state output-feedback Tube MPC, intermittent position packets, SMF/CZ posterior inclusion or terminal information-RCI. Detailed theorem assumptions, exact states/inputs, constraints, sensor types, horizons, solver and computation time: **not confirmed from full text**.
2. José Agnelo Bezerra and Davi A. Santos, *On the guidance of fully-actuated multirotor aerial vehicles under control allocation constraints using the receding-horizon strategy*, ISA Transactions 126 (2022), 21–35. DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isatra.2021.07.019 ; PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34366122/ . **Abstract only, 2026-11-03**. Outer nonlinear receding-horizon guidance and inner inverse-dynamics force/torque controller with convex optimal allocation; abstract reports proofs for inner/outer asymptotic stability, recursive guidance feasibility and allocation feasibility, illustrated with a **fully actuated quadrotor with two-DOF vectorable rotors**. It is not a conventional fixed-axis underactuated four-rotor vehicle. Thus its allocation-feasibility proof cannot be imported into the standard single-direction fixed-axis allocation polytope without rederivation. Exact states, input bounds, measurements, theorem assumptions, solver timings: **unconfirmed**.

Luis Recalde et al., *Constrained Visual Servoing of Quadrotors Based on Model Predictive Control*, IFAC-PapersOnLine 55(37) (2022), 353–360, https://repositorio.uti.edu.ec/items/04944da2-e8b0-46b4-b8e3-b7c65493dc25 . **Abstract only, 2026-11-03**. Image-based visual servo MPC uses field-of-view and bounded control constraints with a velocity-command reduced model identified by DMDc for a commercial Mavic Pro 2, evaluated in Webots versus classic IBVS. The high-level velocity command is not an independently actuated motor force/torque channel; no SMF/Tube safety theorem can be inferred from the abstract. Detailed model dimensions, exact input, solver/measurement timing: **unconfirmed**.

## Cross-paper inference and model-selection decision
The same generic phrase “MPC for quadrotors” refers to materially different closed-loop contracts:
- Santos/Lagoa: robust *outer guidance* with assumed stable inner position/attitude loops; the nominal tube state is a reduced closed-loop translation, not 12-state rigid-body state.
- Bezerra/Santos: allocation-aware *fully actuated vectorable-rotor* platform; allocation feasibility is structurally different from fixed-axis quadrotor.
- Recalde et al.: vision/velocity-command *commercial autopilot* interface, not motor-level optimization.
For a fixed-axis underactuated quadrotor, if the selected virtual input is `u=[T,tau_x,tau_y,tau_z]` and rotor thrust vector `f`, require `u=Gf` and `0<=f<=fmax` (plus applicable rate limits); componentwise bounds on virtual u alone are not a sufficient actuator feasibility test. When considering SMF-conditioned output feedback, additionally verify posterior containment and causally admissible actions for every observation history. These are **our engineering requirements**, not the cited papers' proven results.
**Selection unchanged:** 12-state nonlinear six-DOF simulation plant remains a candidate, 6-state planar dynamics as reduced proof baseline; 4-state finite safety certificates do not imply full closed-loop feasibility. No model is declared finally validated; Gate A blocked.
