Set-Membership Estimation Based Distributed
Cooperative Localization of Multiple UAVs
Yu Ding , Xujie Qin, Yirui Cong , Member, IEEE, and Xiangke Wang , Senior Member, IEEE
Abstract—This paper investigates the distributed cooperative
localization problem for mobile multi-UAV systems using set-
membership estimation (SME). Unlike existing SME approaches
relying on prior-known anchors or beacons, we consider a fully
autonomous scenario where UAVs need to collaboratively derive
guaranteed positional sets using only absolute measurements and
relative range measurements from neighboring agents. To handle
this problem, we first propose a law-of-total-range method to rig-
orously quantify the estimated sets from relative measurements,
which fits the general form of relative measurements between
pairwise UAVs. Based on this new method, a novel distributed SME
framework is developed, where each UAV can obtain the guaran-
teed set containing its true state with only local measurements and
communications. In addition, a distributed localization algorithm
based on extended constrained zonotopes (ECZ) is designed with
a proposed framework. Simulation results and field experiments
verify that our proposed method efficiently improves the coopera-
tive localization accuracy and outperforms the linearization-based
extended SME method.
Index Terms—Set-membership estimation, multi-UAV system,
cooperative localization.
I. INTRODUCTION
IN RECENT years, the cooperative localization problem
of multi-agent systems has received considerable research
attention [1], [2], [3]. Various estimation methods have been
widely used in the cooperative localization problem.
For observer-based methods, a distributed self-localization
algorithm was developed for multi-agent systems by using rel-
ative position measurements in [4]. The results were extended
to the distributed localization problem of sources with bearing
angle measurements in [5]. In [6], distributed consensus filters
were developed for a set of targets to utilize relative velocity
and distance measurements to estimate the relative positions
between UAVs. For stochastic methods, distributed localization
algorithms are often designed based on Bayesian theory [7], [8].

In [13], nonlinear relative measurements have been considered,
where a recursive quadratic filter has been proposed to compute

bounded noise, authors have proposed an H∞-filter-based

ment information directly instead of placing a localization mark
on the robots. It is worth mentioning that the aforementioned
methods have limitations when facing unknown but bounded
(UBB) noises. To the best of our knowledge, the unknown-input
observer (UIO) [16] and the set-membership estimation (SME)
are two effective ways to deal with the UBB noise.
In this paper, we focus on the guaranteed method, i.e., set-
membership estimation (SME), to address the cooperative lo-
calization problem in multi-agent systems. Based on our current
information, only a few articles have focused on studying the
SME based cooperative localization problem. In [17], the au-
thor considered a multi-agent system with both absolute and
relative position measurements. A constrained zonotope [18]
based algorithm was developed to enable distributed cooperative
localization. In [19], the indoor localization problem based on
the received signal strength indicator (RSSI) is considered.
The author proposed a distributed set-membership estimation
method in which the state of the localization system is uncertain
and constrained by a given ellipsoid. In [20], Silvestre investi-
gated the set-membership-based localization problem of relative
range and bearing measurement with a fixed beacon. A new tool
of set description, w.r.t., theconstrainedconvexgenerator (CCG)
is proposed as a generalization of the constrained zonotope
and applied in the localization problem to derive the estimated
guaranteed set. Qin et al. considered the same localization prob-
lem [21], and proposed a moving-horizon extended constrained
zonotopic algorithm to accelerate calculations. References [22]
and [23] studied the SME-based localization problem using GPS

It should be pointed out that existing works on SME-based
localization rely on prior knowledge of anchors or beacons,
which may be unrealistic in practical applications, particularly in
outdoor environments. Moreover, most works handle nonlinear
relative measurements by linearizing simultaneous equations.
This is because the state coupling caused by relative measure-
ments is difficult to decouple and form distributed frameworks.
Thus, in this article, we study the SME-based distributed
cooperative localization problem of a multi-UAV system, where
each UAV aims to generate a guaranteed set that contains its own
position. The main contributions of this paper are summarized
as follows:
r By analyzing uncertain variables, we accurately describe
the uncertain range determined by general relative mea-
surements between two agents, which forms the basis for
establishing the distributed SME framework.
r A distributed SME framework is developed that can handle
kinematic scenarios without prior knowledge of anchors.
With the proposed framework, each UAV can obtain the
guaranteed set that contains its true state using only local
measurements and communications.
r An extended constrained zonotopic distributed localiza-
tion algorithm is designed based on the proposed SME
framework. Simulations verify that the designed algorithm
can effectively improve accuracy with cooperative infor-
mation from relative measurements, and outperforms the
linearization-based extended SME method in [23].

algorithm designed in Section IV. Section V presents simulation
results that corroborate the effectiveness of our methods. Finally,
Section VI provides the concluding remarks of the paper.

VI. CONCLUSION
In this article, we have studied the distributed cooperative
localization problem of a multi-UAV system with absolute and
relative range measurements based on set-membership estima-
tion (SME). Each kinematic UAV needs to realize SME-based
localization with its absolute measurement of itself and relative
range measurements of its neighbors. Firstly, to deal with the
general form of relative measurements between pairwise UAVs,
we have proposed a new method to derive the uncertain range
of uncertain variables with the law of total range. Based on
this, a novel distributed SME framework has been developed,
where each UAV can obtain the guaranteed set containing its
true state with only local measurements and communications.
Furthermore, we have designed an extended constrained zono-
topic localization algorithm as a realization of the proposed
distributed SME framework. Finally, several simulation results
have corroborated the effectiveness of our proposed method,
and our designed algorithm can effectively improve accuracy
with cooperative information from relative measurements, and
has less conservativeness than the linearization-based estimation
method (ESMF and EKF).
For future works, we aim to study the rigid-graph-based
cooperative localization problem of dense with SME, which
can be applied in dense UAV formations. We also consider
the data-driven unknown-input-observer with SME, which can
present reliable guaranteed sets and the convergent pointwise
estimations simultaneously; when the model contains dynamic
level uncertainties, the data-driven unknown-input observer can
deal with the model mismatch, and the presented SME method
can be applied to realize cooperative localization of each UAV.
